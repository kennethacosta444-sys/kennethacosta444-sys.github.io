SaferRingAI call-screening app for iPhone LIVE2026 – now
SaferRing screens unknown callers before your phone rings. People on your contact list go straight through; everyone else gets asked who they are and why they're calling, and you see the reason, a transcript, and any red flags before you answer.
  • Took it from a browser demo to a paid iOS app on the App Store
  • iPhone app: SwiftUI with CallKit, push notifications, and Twilio Voice. Guided setup, screening levels, personal rules, call history, and screened-call details
  • Call backend: TypeScript serverless functions on Netlify verify every Twilio webhook, catch forwarding loops, ask follow-up questions when the caller is vague, then connect, warn, decline, or block
  • Detection: Claude's read on the call is combined with local checks for code requests, payment changes, remote access, and callers claiming to be a company. Those claims get checked against a library of real support numbers and policies
  • Accounts: a separate Node service with Better Auth, PostgreSQL, email verification, 2FA, and encrypted backups. Contact sync matches hashed numbers so names never leave the phone
  • Backed by 170+ automated tests plus an evaluation harness for screening accuracy
